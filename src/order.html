How to Place an Order

Follow these simple steps to grab your favorite food from Campus Bites:

  1. Choose Your Items: Browse our Menu or Deals page to decide what you'd like to eat.
  2. Go to the Counter: Join the queue at the main ordering counter.
  3. Place & Pay: Give your order to the cashier and pay using Cash or Campus Card.
  4. Collect Token: Receive your numbered order token and receipt.
  5. Pick Up: Wait for your token number to be displayed on the screen, then collect your hot food from the Pick-Up Window.

Important Guidelines

Peak Hours

Expect longer waiting times between 12:30 PM and 2:00 PM due to lunch breaks.

Pick-Up Rules

Please do not crowd the pick-up area. Wait until your token number is clearly announced or displayed.
